How we measure
Width
The long side of the top, at its widest point.
Depth
The short side of the top, front to back.
Height
From the floor to the top surface, the height you put a cup down at.
Thickness
The top slab only, in mm.

At home: Measure the gap under your sofa: the base has to slide in. Then measure the seat height.
